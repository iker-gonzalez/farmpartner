● YOUR DATA · PLAIN WORDS

Your field, your number, your call.

FarmPartner needs to know where your field is to read it from space, and your number to reply to you. Here is exactly what we keep, who sees what, and how to make it go away.

  • Your number stays with us

    We never sell, rent or share your phone number. It is used only to reply to you on WhatsApp or SMS, and it is never sent to the AI, to NASA or to any data service.

  • Locations travel without names

    To fetch satellite and weather data we send only the field’s coordinates to public services (NASA, USDA, Open‑Meteo). They never receive your number, your name or anything else that identifies you.

  • The AI never sees who you are

    Claude, the AI behind the advice, gets your messages and your field’s data, labelled with the county or town — not your phone number.

  • Reports behind a secret link

    Each field report lives at a random, unguessable address. It shows the area and the analysis — never your number or your name.

  • No ads, no data sales

    We don’t show ads and we don’t sell data. What you tell us about your field is used to give you better advice, and nothing else.

  • Leave whenever you want

    Text STOP and we won’t message you first again. Text RESET to erase your fields. Write to us and we’ll delete everything.

The fine print, still in plain words

What we keep

Your phone number, language, your field’s location and crops, your goals, the conversation, and anything you report (rain, pests, yields). We keep them together so the bot remembers your field next time you write.

What we don’t keep

Raw satellite images. We store only the summaries computed for your field (rainfall, soil, vegetation trends), not the original NASA files.

Where it lives

In a private Postgres database (Supabase) that only the FarmPartner server, which runs on Google Cloud in Europe, can read. Public access to its tables is switched off.

Deleting your data

Text RESET to remove your fields and their history. To erase your number and conversation as well, e‑mail {{ email }} from anywhere, with the number you used.
