● YOUR DATA · PLAIN WORDS

Your field, your number, your call.

FarmPartner needs to know where your field is to read it from space, and your number to reply to you. Here is exactly what we keep, who sees what, and how to make it go away.

  • Your number stays with us

    We never sell, rent or share your phone number. It is used only to reply to you on WhatsApp or SMS, and it is never sent to the AI, to NASA or to any data service.

  • Locations travel without names

    To fetch satellite and weather data we send the field’s coordinates — or the place name you type — to public services (NASA, USDA, Open‑Meteo). They never receive your number or your name.

  • The AI never sees who you are

    Claude, the AI behind the advice, gets your messages and your field’s data, labelled with the county or town — not your phone number. Phone numbers, e‑mails and exact coordinates you type are removed before it reads your messages, and its web searches for farming facts never include your details.

  • Reports behind a secret link

    Each field report lives at a random, unguessable address. It shows the area and the analysis — never your number or your name.

  • No ads, no data sales

    We don’t show ads and we don’t sell data. What you tell us about your field is used to give you better advice, and nothing else.

  • Leave whenever you want

    Text STOP and we won’t message you first again. Text RESET to erase your fields and the locations you sent. Write to us and we’ll delete everything.

The fine print, still in plain words

What we keep

Your phone number, language, your field’s location and crops, your goals, the conversation (voice notes as their written text), and anything you report (rain, pests, yields). We keep them together so the bot remembers your field next time you write.

What we don’t keep

Raw satellite images: we store only the summaries computed for your field (rainfall, soil, vegetation trends). Nor the photos and voice recordings you send: we read them and keep only the text.

How long we keep it

As long as you keep writing. After 12 months without a message from you, we delete your number, your fields and the conversation automatically. Spoken replies are stored as audio for 30 days, so WhatsApp can deliver them, then deleted.

Where it lives

In a private Postgres database (Supabase, in Ireland) that only the FarmPartner server, which runs on Google Cloud in Belgium, can read. Public access to its tables is switched off.

Deleting your data

Text RESET to remove your fields, their history and the locations you sent. To erase your number and conversation as well, e‑mail {{ email }} from anywhere, with the number you used.

Who is responsible

The FarmPartner team, which built it for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 in Bilbao, Spain. Write to {{ email }} about anything on this page.

Why we use your data

Only to answer you and, if you want them, to send you weather alerts for your field. You ask for this by writing to FarmPartner, and you can stop at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Write to us and we reply within a month. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

Outside Europe

Twilio, Anthropic and Google are U.S. companies, so what they receive (listed above) leaves the EU. They handle it under their data processing terms for European users, which include the EU’s standard contractual clauses.
