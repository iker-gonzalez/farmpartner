● WHAT & HOW

Satellite data, AI and farmers’ know-how, working for your field.

FarmPartner is an AI engine that helps farmers adapt to climate change and make better decisions about their land, starting with crop rotation. It cross-references NASA Earth observation data, information about each farm and the practical knowledge of experienced farmers, and delivers personalized recommendations through a chatbot on the messaging apps farmers already use. It is built for generational renewal, passing that know-how on to the next generation of farmers; for digital inclusion, since anyone can use it by voice or by text; and with no language barriers, in {{ langs.text }} languages.

00THE CHALLENGE

The climate has already changed. Decisions in the field haven’t yet.

FarmPartner was born from the Field Shift: Adapting Farms with NASA Data challenge of the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026: helping farmers adapt their land to climate change with NASA’s open data.

THE PROBLEM

  • Intensive farming wears out the land

    Planting the same crop year after year exhausts the soil: it loses nutrients and organic matter, pests and erosion increase, and every season yields less. Crop rotation is essential to let the land recover, but choosing the right rotation means knowing each field’s climate, soil and water.

  • The climate is changing faster than experience

    Rain, frost and heat waves no longer follow the usual calendar. What worked for decades is no longer reliable.

  • The data exists, but it doesn’t reach the field

    NASA has been observing every plot on the planet for more than 20 years. But that data comes in technical formats, in English, and is made for scientists.

  • Knowledge is being lost

    Many experienced farmers retire with no one to take over, and what they know is never written down.

  • Technology leaves many behind

    Farming tools are often complex apps, in a single language, that require reading charts or being comfortable with technology.

WHAT WE SOLVE

  • Turning data into decisions

    We have built an AI engine that reads NASA data for each field and turns it into a concrete crop rotation that brings the soil back to life, with its benefits and its risks.

  • Bringing science to the chat

    Anyone can talk to the AI engine from the messaging apps they already use, with nothing to install. It works on WhatsApp today, and soon on Messenger, iMessage, Instagram, Line and SMS.

  • Generational renewal

    The knowledge of experienced farmers feeds the recommendations and reaches the next generation.

  • Digital inclusion

    Just type or send a voice note. No need to read charts or be comfortable with technology.

  • No language barriers

    Every farmer talks to FarmPartner in their own language: {{ langs.text }} languages.

  • Support all season long

    We send frost, heat and drought alerts when conditions change.

01WHAT IT DOES

Advice that fits your field, not a region’s average.

FarmPartner looks at the environmental conditions of each area (temperature, rainfall, soil moisture, vegetation health and water availability) to identify suitable crops and recommend rotation strategies. Farmers can compare what they do today with alternatives, see the likely benefits and risks, and get alerts when conditions change.

SOURCE 01

NASA Earth observation

Two decades of satellite records on climate, soil moisture, vegetation and water, for any field on the planet.

SOURCE 02

Your farm

Location, water, current crops and priorities, plus your corrections when the data doesn’t match what you see in the field.

SOURCE 03

Farmers’ experience

Practical knowledge on crops, soils, planting windows and regional conditions, organized into a knowledge base the AI consults.

02HOW IT WORKS

From a location pin to a rotation you can trust.

An integrated process: collect what matters about your farm, analyze the data, and turn it into recommendations you can act on.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your farm

    Share basic information about your land: location, water availability, current crops and farming priorities. You can also correct or adjust the environmental data so it better reflects the real conditions of your field.

  2. 02

    We read NASA data for your field

    The platform brings together several NASA datasets, harmonizes them and adds local weather forecasts, combining long-term environmental patterns with short-term information.

    See the datasets ↓
  3. 03

    AI analyzes and recommends

    AI weighs the environmental data alongside your farm’s details to identify suitable crops and propose alternative rotation strategies. You get your current rotation compared with at least two alternatives, with explanations, supporting data, expected benefits, possible risks and the data gaps to keep in mind.

    See a sample report ↓
  4. 04

    Farmer knowledge, built in

    Experienced farmers contribute practical know-how on crops, soils, planting periods and regional conditions. It is organized into a knowledge base the AI consults, so every recommendation combines scientific evidence with real field experience.

  5. 05

    Ongoing support

    FarmPartner sends weather alerts, spots chances to make better use of the land between harvests and suggests cover crops or alternative uses such as grazing. Just ask the chatbot, by text or voice note, whenever you need it.

  6. 06

    Global knowledge exchange

    NASA data covers the whole planet, so FarmPartner can connect farming communities facing similar climates. Through climate-analogue analysis, farmers discover regions already living the conditions their own area may face in the future, and learn from how they adapted.

04NASA DATA

Five NASA missions, read for one field.

Each dataset answers a different question about your land. Together, and alongside local forecasts, they describe the past and near future of your field’s climate.

CLIMATE & RAIN

NASA POWER

20 years of rain, temperature and root-zone wetness: evaporation demand, frost dates, heat-stress and heavy-rain days.

VEGETATION

MODIS NDVI

How green the field is through the year, and the months the soil lies bare.

SOIL

SMAP

Root-zone and surface soil moisture now versus earlier months.

CROP STRESS

ECOSTRESS

Field-scale (70 m) crop water stress: whether the crop is short of water.

WATER

GRACE · GRACE-FO

Long-term trends in water storage, groundwater included.

AND MORE

Every source →

USDA crop and soil maps, FAO, Open-Meteo, 3,663 field trials, long-term experiments and a library of farm handbooks.
